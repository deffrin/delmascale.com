WORKSHOP FOR MAINTENANCE AND SERVICES

WORKSHOP FOR CALIBRATION, REPAIR AND MAINTENANCE SERVICES OF WEIGHING SCALES

Balances should always be checked with reference weights you can rely on and trust.
We calibrate, adjust and document the results in a calibration certificate. The calibration services cover the basic reporting of conventional mass correction, uncertainty and traceability information in accordance with OIML-1994 requirements.

We undertake servicing and repair of all types of weighing scales and equipment.
We have a team of well-trained sales & service engineers who have thorough experience in servicing all types of weighing scales and systems with minimum cost & absolutely or no downtime.

We specialise in maintaining, servicing and calibrating all types & brands of weighing machines and systems of various capacities and applications.
We also install and commission all types of weighbridges & nonstandard weighing equipment.
We undertake an Annual Maintenance contract scheme for all types of weighing equipment including Batching Plants.
We have all the necessary OEM spares to maintain these weighing scales mainly load cells and indicators.
